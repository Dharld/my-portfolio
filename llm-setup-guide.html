Option A: Groq (RECOMMENDED - Super Fast & Reliable)

✅ Lightning fast responses
✅ Very reliable
✅ 100% FREE
✅ No credit card required
Step 1: Go to console.groq.com
Step 2: Sign up with your email (free)
Step 3: Click "API Keys" → "Create API Key"
Step 4: Copy your key and open assets/js/chatbot.js
Step 5: Replace this line:
API_KEY: 'gsk_your_free_groq_key_here',
with your actual key:
API_KEY: 'gsk_your_actual_key_here',

Option B: HuggingFace (Alternative)

✅ Also FREE
✅ No credit card required
⚠️ Slightly slower than Groq
Step 1: Go to huggingface.co/settings/tokens
Step 2: Click "New token" → "Read" access → Create
Step 3: In chatbot.js, uncomment the HuggingFace section and add your key
